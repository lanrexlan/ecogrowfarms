About EcoGrow
Farms Limited

A technology-enabled Nigerian agribusiness established to produce, source, market, and distribute quality farm products while improving productivity, sustainability, and food supply systems.

Who We Are

An integrated
Nigerian agribusiness.

EcoGrow Farms Limited is a Nigerian agribusiness focused on crop production, livestock and poultry, agricultural sourcing, marketing, distribution, and farm-to-market supply systems. The company was established to contribute to food security by producing and supplying high-quality agricultural products including fruits, vegetables, grains, livestock, poultry, and other farm products.

We are building an operation that combines modern technology with disciplined farming practice — addressing every stage of the agricultural value chain from field production to final-mile delivery. Our approach is designed not just for what the market demands today, but for what compliance-ready buyers, institutional partners, and consumers will increasingly require tomorrow.

EcoGrow is registered and operates in Nigeria, with a focus on building the infrastructure, documentation systems, and supply relationships that position Nigerian agriculture for growth on both domestic and international stages.

  • Livestock & poultry systems
  • Agricultural sourcing & off-take
  • Post-harvest handling & distribution
  • Farm-to-market supply systems
  • Digital records & traceability

What Makes EcoGrow Different

Built differently,
from the ground up.

Most agribusinesses in Nigeria operate in silos — focusing on one stage of the value chain while leaving others to chance. EcoGrow is designed differently: integrated, documented, and technology-informed at every step.

01 ⚙️
Technology-Enabled Agriculture

We embed technology into farming operations — not as a marketing layer, but as a working infrastructure. From mechanized field operations and drip irrigation to greenhouse systems and digital farm records, technology improves our visibility, reduces waste, and builds the data trail that accountable supply requires.

02 🌱
Sustainable Crop and Livestock Production

Sustainability at EcoGrow means producing in ways that are economically durable and environmentally responsible. Soil-aware input programs, water-efficient irrigation, biosecurity protocols for livestock, and responsible post-harvest practices — built to last, not just to yield.

03 🚛
Farm-to-Market Supply Thinking

We think about the full journey from seed to sale. Post-harvest handling, cold-chain planning, grading standards, and delivery logistics are built into how we operate — because what happens after harvest determines whether great farming translates into great supply.

04 📋
Quality-Focused and Traceability-Ready Systems

We are building documented production protocols aligned with Nigerian regulatory standards, including SON frameworks and NAQS compliance requirements. Every batch is assigned origin references, input logs, and handling records — building the accountability infrastructure that institutional buyers and serious partners require.

Our Approach

Modern tools,
disciplined practice.

EcoGrow combines traditional agricultural knowledge with modern tools and systems. We do not chase technology for its own sake — we adopt what actually improves production outcomes, reduces post-harvest losses, and makes our supply more reliable and traceable.

This means mechanized farming where it reduces labor intensity and improves consistency. Drip irrigation where it conserves water and extends growing seasons. Digital records where they build the documentation trail that institutional relationships require.

Stage 01
Precision Field Planning
Soil testing, improved seed selection, input optimization, and mechanized planting — operations designed around what the land actually needs.
Stage 02
Climate-Smart Production
Drip irrigation, greenhouse and net-house structures, and weather-aware scheduling keep production consistent regardless of seasonal variation.
Stage 03
Digital Record Keeping
Input logs, spray records, livestock treatments, and harvest volumes captured digitally — creating the data trail compliance-ready buyers require.
Stage 04
Post-Harvest Handling
Structured grading standards, temperature-appropriate storage, and cold-chain planning to reduce losses and maintain quality from farm gate to market.
Stage 05
Organized Distribution
Route-optimized delivery with batch documentation — connecting farm supply to wholesale, retail, institutional, and processing buyers.
Stage 06
Traceable Accountability
Full field-to-end-customer traceability: origin records, handling documentation, and compliance notes that underpin credible, lasting supply relationships.
