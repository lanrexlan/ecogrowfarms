Intelligence Products

Software born
from the field.

Every product we build started as a problem we faced on our own farm. We build tools we actually use — then open them to governments, agencies, cooperatives, and insurers who face the same problems at greater scale. Most of them run on satellite and radar data alone, which means they deploy anywhere we can draw a plot boundary.

Active
CropSentinel
AI crop failure prediction — 6 to 8 weeks ahead

Satellite imagery and an LSTM model predict farm-level yield failure before it happens. The base tier runs on orbital data alone — nothing installed on the ground. SMS alerts to farmers, risk dashboards to agencies, parametric triggers to insurers.

  • Satellite-only tier — no hardware required
  • Farm-level yield loss probability
  • Farmer SMS alert in local language
  • Agri-insurance API trigger
Beta
SoilPulse
Continuous soil intelligence for precision input decisions

Solar IoT soil probes tracking NPK, pH, moisture, and compaction across farm clusters in real time — feeding a prescription engine that tells extension officers exactly what each field needs.

  • Real-time NPK, pH, moisture data
  • Fertiliser prescription engine
  • Extension officer and agri-dealer portal
  • Seasonal trend analysis
Beta
HarvestIQ
Market price intelligence for farmers and cooperatives

Aggregates real-time price signals from commodity markets, wholesale hubs, and rural networks — giving cooperatives and offtakers a data edge on when and where to sell.

  • Multi-market price aggregation
  • Sell-timing recommendations
  • Cooperative and offtaker portals
  • SMS price alerts for field agents
In Development
FloodSentinel
Radar flood forecasting and 48-hour damage mapping

Sentinel-1 radar sees through rainy-season cloud that blinds optical satellites. Ward-level inundation forecasts days ahead, then cropland damage quantified within 48 hours of the event.

  • All-weather radar — works through cloud
  • Ward-level inundation risk maps
  • 48-hour post-event damage assessment
  • Parametric flood insurance triggers
In Development
OriginProof
EUDR deforestation-free compliance, proven from orbit

Automated per-plot compliance dossiers for exporters of cocoa, palm, sesame and rubber — forest-loss history against the 2020 baseline, generated retroactively from public satellite archives.

  • Forest-loss change detection since 2020
  • Regulator-ready PDF and JSON dossiers
  • Consignment-level compliance roll-up
  • No site visit, works retroactively
In Development
FieldMap
Plot boundaries, crop type and area from orbit

Automatic field delineation and crop classification from Sentinel time series — the verified plot register that subsidy programmes, insurers and lenders are all missing.

  • Automatic sub-hectare plot delineation
  • Crop type by growth signature
  • Registry reconciliation and ghost-plot flags
  • Base layer for CropSentinel and OriginProof
Coming Soon
IrrigateAI
AI irrigation scheduling that cuts water waste by half

Combines satellite evapotranspiration data, local weather forecasts, and SoilPulse readings to generate daily irrigation schedules for farms and irrigation scheme operators — no agronomist visit required.

  • Daily AI-generated irrigation schedules
  • Evapotranspiration and weather data fusion
  • Drip and centre-pivot controller API
  • Water use reduction reporting for compliance
  • Integrates with SoilPulse sensor network
How It Works

From the field to a decision
in time to act.

01 — SENSE 🛰
Sense

Satellites pass overhead daily. Our IoT probes measure what satellites can't see underground. Both streams feed continuously into one data pipeline.

02 — MODEL 🧠
Model

Our LSTM AI processes the combined stream, compares against historical African crop stress patterns, and outputs farm-level yield probabilities with confidence scores.

03 — ALERT 📡
Alert

Farmers receive an SMS. Agencies see a district risk map. Insurers receive a parametric trigger. Everyone gets the signal they need in the channel they actually use.

04 — RESPOND 🌍
Respond

Aid is pre-positioned before roads flood. Irrigate before the crop fails. Sell before the price drops. Six to eight weeks is enough for every stakeholder to act.
