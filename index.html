Quality & Traceability

Building the documentation infrastructure that trust requires.

At EcoGrow, quality is not a marketing word — it is a system. We are building documented production protocols aligned with Nigerian regulatory standards, including SON frameworks and NAQS compliance requirements for produce handling and export readiness.

Our goal is a supply chain where every batch can be traced: what field it came from, what inputs were applied, how it was handled post-harvest, and where it was delivered. That level of accountability is what modern buyers, institutional partners, and regulators increasingly demand.

We describe our produce as sustainably grown, quality-focused, and building traceability — because these reflect what we are actually doing rather than certifications we have not yet earned.
