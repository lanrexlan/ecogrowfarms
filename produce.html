Section 01

Staple Crops for
Food Security

EcoGrow cultivates and supports the production of strategic Nigerian food crops such as maize, cassava, yam, rice, and grains. These crops are central to household consumption, food processing, animal feed, and national food security.

Our staple crop programme prioritises reliable, high-volume output that serves both the immediate domestic market and the supply chains of processors, aggregators, and institutional buyers who require consistent quality and documentation.

Technology Approach

Our crop production model is designed to use mechanized land preparation, improved planting methods, irrigation where suitable, pest management, and digital farm records to improve consistency and productivity across all staple crop activities.

Section 02

Fresh Vegetables Grown
With Smarter Systems

Vegetables require careful handling, water management, pest control, and fast movement to market. EcoGrow is positioned to use protected farming methods such as greenhouse and screenhouse systems where suitable, helping improve vegetable quality, reduce exposure to harsh weather, and support more reliable production cycles.

Technology Approach

Greenhouse and protected farming systems support better water control, improved pest management, cleaner production environments, and more predictable output — enabling year-round vegetable supply with reduced weather dependency.

Why Protected Farming
Consistent quality through weather-resilient systems

Section 04

Sourcing Quality
Farm Produce

Beyond direct production, EcoGrow also sources agricultural produce from trusted farmers, cooperatives, and supply partners. This allows us to strengthen supply, support rural producers, and deliver larger volumes to wholesalers, retailers, processors, and institutional buyers.

Our sourcing model is built on consistent standards: produce that meets our quality thresholds, documented origin, and handling that aligns with how we would manage our own farm output. Working with partners who share our commitment to quality is as important as what we grow ourselves.

Our Approach to Aggregation

We work with cooperatives and smallholder networks to consolidate supply, provide technical guidance on post-harvest handling, and create structured off-take arrangements that give producers reliable market access while giving our buyers consistent volume.

🤝
Cooperative Partnerships
Structured off-take agreements with farmer cooperatives, providing them with reliable buyers and us with scalable supply.
👨‍🌾
Smallholder Networks
Supporting small-scale producers with market access, technical input, and post-harvest guidance in exchange for quality-assured supply.
🏭

🏢
Institutional Buyers
Hospitals, schools, hotels, and government food programmes seeking reliable supply with proper documentation and traceable origin.
🛒
Wholesale & Retail
Fresh produce at graded quality for wholesale markets, supermarkets, and premium retail food channels.
